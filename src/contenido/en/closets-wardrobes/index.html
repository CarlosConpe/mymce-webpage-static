Custom Closets and Wardrobes in San Luis Potosí

MYMCE designs and builds made-to-measure closets, wardrobes and walk-in closets in its San Luis Potosí factory, as it has since 1987. We serve homes in San Luis Potosí, Soledad de Graciano Sánchez, Villa de Pozos and the rest of the state.

If a bedroom has an empty niche or just a rod for clothes, we can fill it with a built-in closet planned around what you actually keep: long and short hanging, drawers, shelves, shoe racks and trouser racks. If you have a spare room or a large dressing area, we can turn it into a walk-in closet.

From the first message to installation

1. Appointment

Message us on WhatsApp, call or email. Tell us which rooms need a closet and we agree on a date to meet.

2. On-site measurement

We visit your home and measure each niche or room, so the closet is designed for the space you have.

3. Design

We plan the interior layout, doors and finishes with you. Production starts once you approve the design.

4. Manufacturing

We build your closets with CNC machinery at our own plant in San Luis Potosí, where we have made furniture since 1987.

5. Installation

Our installers fit the closets in your home and adjust doors and drawers so they run smoothly.
