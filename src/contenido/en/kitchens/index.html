Custom Kitchens in San Luis Potosí

We have built made-to-measure kitchens in our San Luis Potosí factory since 1987. We serve homes in San Luis Potosí, Soledad de Graciano Sánchez, Villa de Pozos and the rest of the state.

Whether your house came with a basic kitchen or you are renovating, we design and build a new one around your space and the way you cook, then install it. Because we are the manufacturer, the design, the production and the installation stay with one team from the first visit to the last adjustment.

From the first message to installation

1. Appointment

Message us on WhatsApp, call or email. Tell us about the kitchen you want and we agree on a date to meet.

2. On-site measurement

We come to your home and measure the room, including walls, windows, plumbing and outlets, so the design matches the house.

3. Design

Our designers prepare the layout, materials and finishes with you. Production starts once you approve the design.

4. Manufacturing

We build your kitchen with CNC machinery at our own plant in San Luis Potosí, where we have made furniture since 1987, and we check the finishes on every piece.

5. Installation

Our installers mount the cabinets and fronts in your home and adjust each piece to the space.
