Custom Kitchens & Closets in San Luis Potosí

MYMCE has designed and built kitchens, closets and walk-in closets for homes in San Luis Potosí for almost 40 years. If you moved here to work at one of the industrial parks and want cabinetry made for your house, we measure your space, design it with you, build it in our own plant and install it.

Message us on WhatsAppSee real projects
We are a carpentry shop and factory, so the people who measure your kitchen are the same team that cuts and assembles it. We work in houses across San Luis Potosí, Soledad de Graciano Sánchez and Villa de Pozos, including new homes in developments such as Catara, Sirenia, Valle Real and Imperia. Every piece is made to the plan of your home, whether it is a new build or a renovation.
